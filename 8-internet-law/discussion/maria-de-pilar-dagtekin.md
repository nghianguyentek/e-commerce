Legal frameworks and regulatory organizations contribute to creating safe and trustworthy online business environments by establishing standards that protect consumers and hold businesses accountable for their practices. One example of this is the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which protects the personal information of individuals in the European Union. The GDPR requires that businesses have a lawful basis for processing personal data and gives consumers greater control over how their information is collected, used, and stored (European Parliament & Council of the European Union, 2016).  I think that these protections encourage accountability and help consumers feel more confident shopping online.
Intellectual property protections such as copyright, trademarks, and patents influence ethical and fair online business practices by preventing unauthorized use of creative works, protecting brand identities, and encouraging innovation (LawShelf, n.d.). One real example of a company and legal case that demonstrates the importance of protecting digital content in e-commerce is Tiffany v. eBay (2010), which involved counterfeit Tiffany & Co. products that were sold online through eBay. The case highlighted the importance of trademark protection and marketplace accountability in maintaining fair competition.

One ethical or legal challenge that arises when businesses use new technologies such as AI or data-driven algorithms to influence consumer behavior is the potential misuse of personal information to manipulate purchasing decisions. Things like personalized ads or recommendations can encourage over spending or exploit consumer vulnerabilities such as financial insecurity, impulsive spending habits, or emotional triggers by using personalized advertisements and recommendations to influence their purchasing decisions. Businesses should respond responsibly by being transparent about data collection, letting consumers control their information, and ensuring algorithms respect privacy and autonomy rather than prioritizing profits.
