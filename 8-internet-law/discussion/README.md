# Discussion Unit 8

This assignment will assess your knowledge and skills about:

- Identify laws and organizations that regulate online business activities.
- Explain how copyright, trademark, and patent laws protect digital content.
- Describe basic ethical and legal challenges in online commerce.
- Recognize how new technologies influence business and consumer behavior.
- Evaluate responsible and sustainable practices in e-commerce.

## Task

Using this unit’s readings and at least one real example (company, legal case, or policy), respond to the following questions:

1. How do legal frameworks and regulatory organizations contribute to creating safe and trustworthy online business environments? Choose one law or body (e.g., GDPR, CCPA, WIPO, FTC) and briefly explain its key role in protecting consumers or regulating online business activities.
2. How do intellectual property protections such as copyright, trademarks, or patents influence ethical and fair online business practices? Provide one real example of a company or legal case that demonstrates the importance of protecting digital content in e-commerce.
3. Drawing on the readings, describe one ethical or legal challenge that arises when businesses use new technologies — such as AI, data-driven algorithms, or IoT — to influence consumer behavior. How should businesses respond to this challenge responsibly?
