# Legal, Ethical, and Technological Challenges in Digital Business

Oct 27, 2026 03:57

## Question 1: Legal Frameworks and Online Business

Legal frameworks help create safer online business environments by protecting consumer privacy, preventing fraud, and holding businesses accountable. For example, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) establishes rules for collecting, processing, and storing personal data in the European Union. It requires businesses to have a lawful basis for processing personal information and to respect individuals’ privacy rights. These requirements help build consumer trust and encourage responsible data management (European Parliament & Council of the European Union, 2016).

## Question 2: Intellectual Property Protection

Intellectual property laws promote fair competition by protecting original content, brand identities, and inventions. Copyright protects digital content, trademarks distinguish brands, and patents protect qualifying inventions. For example, Amazon uses intellectual property policies to address counterfeit products and protect customers from misleading listings. Such protection helps businesses maintain their reputations while ensuring that customers can make informed purchasing decisions.

## Question 3: Ethical Challenges of Emerging Technologies

Artificial intelligence (AI) and data-driven algorithms allow businesses to personalize advertisements and recommend products based on consumer behavior. However, these technologies can threaten privacy when companies collect excessive personal information or use data without adequate transparency. Ronteau et al. (2023) discuss ethical concerns associated with attention-driven and data-driven business models. To address these challenges, businesses should collect only necessary data, explain how information is used, obtain consent where required, and regularly evaluate algorithms for bias. Responsible technology use can protect consumers while supporting sustainable business growth.
