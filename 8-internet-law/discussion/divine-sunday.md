## Legal, Ethical, and Technological Issues in E-Commerce

### Question 1: Legal Frameworks and Trustworthy Online Businesses

By safeguarding customers' private information, stopping fraud, and establishing accountability, legal frameworks contribute to the development of secure and reliable online business environments. One legal framework that controls how businesses gather, handle, and store personal data is the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Businesses must respect people's right to privacy and have legitimate reasons for processing personal data (European Parliament & Council of the European Union, 2016). These regulations promote openness and assist users of online platforms in making knowledgeable decisions.

### Question 2: Intellectual Property and Ethical Online Business Practices

By prohibiting companies from using other people's creative works, brand identities, or inventions without permission, intellectual property protections encourage fair competition. While trademarks safeguard brand identifiers like names and logos, copyright protects original digital content (Solomons et al., 2022). For instance, in a case involving MetaBirkins non-fungible tokens (NFTs), a U.S. court determined that artist Mason Rothschild was liable for trademark infringement in Hermès International v. Rothschild. The case shows that companies and creators are not always exempt from trademark law just because products are presented in digital environments. Upholding intellectual property rights preserves a brand's reputation and fosters consumer trust.

### Question 3: Ethical Challenges of Emerging Technologies

The gathering and use of personal data to sway consumer behavior is one ethical issue related to artificial intelligence (AI) and data-driven algorithms. To deliver targeted ads, businesses may examine past browsing and purchasing behaviors. While personalization can enhance customer experiences, excessive data collection and opaque algorithms have the potential to compromise privacy and manipulate susceptible customers (Ronteau et al., 2023). Companies should implement clear data policies, secure consumer information, minimize data collection, secure consent, and offer relevant options for tailored advertising. These methods balance corporate goals with consumer rights while promoting responsible innovation.
