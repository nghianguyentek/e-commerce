# Unit 8: Internet Law

In this unit, you will explore the legal, ethical, and regulatory foundations that guide online business activities in the digital world. You will learn about key laws and organizations that govern e-commerce, how intellectual property protections such as copyright, trademarks, and patents safeguard digital content, and why ethical decision-making is essential in online environments. The unit also examines how emerging technologies influence business operations and consumer behavior.

As you progress through this unit, consider the following questions:

- What laws and regulatory bodies play the biggest role in shaping online business operations?
- How do copyright, trademark, and patent protections support fairness and innovation in digital markets?
- In what ways do new technologies create both opportunities and ethical challenges for businesses and consumers?

These competencies will help you understand how legal compliance, ethical practices, and responsible innovation contribute to safe, sustainable, and trustworthy e-commerce experiences for all users.
