# Reading Assignment Unit 8

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:

- How do laws, regulations, and international bodies help make online business environments safer and more trustworthy
- Why are intellectual property protections such as copyright, trademark, and patents essential for digital businesses?
- In what ways do emerging technologies—like AI, IoT, and blockchain—reshape consumer behavior, business models, and ethical practices?
- How can businesses adopt responsible and sustainable practices to build long-term trust in digital markets?
