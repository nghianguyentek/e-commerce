# Unit 5: Digital Markets, Servicing, Dynamic Pricing, and Electronic Payments

In this unit, you will explore how electronic payment systems, digital security, and evolving pricing strategies shape the way transactions occur in e-commerce environments. You will learn about common online payment methods, the importance of secure transactions, and how businesses use dynamic pricing to stay competitive. The unit also introduces the basics of blockchain and cryptocurrencies and highlights key legal and ethical considerations that guide safe and trustworthy digital payment practices.

As you progress through this unit, consider the following questions:

- What makes certain electronic payment systems more suitable for online shopping than others?
- How do security measures such as encryption and authentication protect customers during online transactions?
- How do businesses use dynamic pricing strategies to stay competitive and respond to demand in online markets?
- Why are blockchain and cryptocurrencies increasingly relevant to modern e-commerce payment ecosystems?
- Why is it important for businesses to understand legal and ethical requirements when handling digital payments?

These competencies will help you understand how secure, efficient, and transparent payment processes contribute to trust, reliability, and overall success in e-commerce.
