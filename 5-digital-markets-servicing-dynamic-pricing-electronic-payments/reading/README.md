# Reading Assignment Unit 5

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:

- What makes certain electronic payment systems more suitable and convenient for online shoppers?
- How do online security measures such as encryption and authentication protect customers during digital transactions?
- How do businesses use dynamic pricing strategies to stay competitive and respond to demand in online markets?
- Why are blockchain and cryptocurrencies increasingly relevant to modern e-commerce payment ecosystems?
- What legal and ethical responsibilities do businesses have when collecting and processing customer payment information online?
