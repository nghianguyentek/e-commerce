# Assignment Activity Unit 5

In this assignment, you will examine how electronic payment systems, security mechanisms, and emerging technologies influence trust, efficiency, and customer experience in e-commerce. You will apply key concepts from this unit to evaluate an existing digital payment process used by a real or hypothetical online business.

## Task

Choose one e-commerce company or platform (examples include Amazon, Shopify, PayPal, Myntra, Alibaba, or any local online retailer). Write a 500–700-word reflective response addressing the following:

1. Evaluation of Payment Systems
   - Describe the payment methods offered by the chosen company (e.g., credit/debit cards, mobile wallets, UPI, PayPal, BNPL, etc.).
   - Explain how these payment systems support the company’s online operations.
   - Discuss whether the platform uses any form of dynamic pricing and how this affects customers.
2. Security, Trust, and Emerging Technologies
   - Identify the key security measures used to protect user data and online transactions (e.g., SSL, encryption, OTP, 2FA).
   - Reflect on whether the company incorporates blockchain or cryptocurrency options, and if so, how they impact security and transparency.
   - Discuss at least one legal or ethical consideration the company must follow when handling digital payments.

## Submission Instructions

- Read the rubric on how your assignment will be graded. 
- Your assignment should be a minimum of 500 words and not more than 700 words (not including the reference list or the title); double-spaced in Times New Roman font, which is no greater than 12 points in size. 
- You should use the course readings when creating your assignment. You may also locate and use additional sources provided you cite them according to APA with a correct and clickable link. 
- Remember to cite and list references to validate statements of fact or information that is not considered common knowledge and to corroborate personal opinions/points of view.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Evaluation of Payment Systems: Provides a clear, detailed evaluation of all major payment methods used by the selected company and explains their importance in e-commerce. Includes insightful discussion of dynamic pricing and its impact on customers.
- Security, Trust, and Emerging Technologies: Provides a thorough explanation of key security measures (e.g., encryption, authentication), clearly linking them to customer trust. Includes well-reasoned discussion on blockchain/crypto use and legal or ethical issues.
- Use Information effectively: Chooses from a variety of information sources appropriate to the scope of the question. Selects sources after considering the importance to the question/topic of the multiple criteria used (such as relevance to the question, currency, authority, audience, and bias or point of view).
- Sources and Evidence: Demonstrates skillful use of high-quality, credible, relevant sources to develop ideas appropriate for the discipline and genre of the writing. Proper citations and references are consistently included.
- Effective Communication: Uses graceful language that skillfully communicates meaning to readers with clarity and fluency and is error-free.
- Word Count and Formatting: Meets the word count range, is double-spaced in Times New Roman font, and font is no greater than 12 points in size.
