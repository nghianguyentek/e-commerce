# Self-Quiz Unit 5 Solution

1. The course of action of orders C.O.D, delivery with invoice, payment upon delivery (payment to delivery service provider), delivery confirmed, and delivery service provider transfers funds to merchant's bank is best described as:
   - payment per cash on delivery
2. The course of action of order, invoicing, payment acceptance, delivery, and confirming invoice is best described as:
   - payment per credit card
3. A website that allows customers to find the address and telephone numbers of people they know is an example of:
   - service industrialization
4. Job recruiting sites are examples of e-commerce's effect of:
   - homogenization of time and searching
5. The course of action of order, delivery, sending an invoice, payment, and confirmation is best described as:
   - payment per invoice
6. The course of action of order, sending an invoice, payment, delivery after payment, confirmation is best described as:
   - payment per cash in advance
7. (Multichoice) The characteristics that makes services different from most other products includes:
   - perishability
   - intangibility
   - simultaneity
   - heterogeneity
8. Quality text, audio, and video of a theme park on its website is an example of:
   - using the web to make tangible, the intangible
9. Online, immediate status updates from Airlines about their flights is an example of e-commerce's effect of:
   - homogenization of time and routinization
10. (Multichoice) Intermediaries in a distribution process provide the following functions:
    - adjusting discrepancies of assortments
    - routinize transactions
    - structuring information
11. (Multichoice) The efforts of technology on distribution, networks, and processes include:
    - eliminating the effect of distance
    - homogenizing time
    - making location non-relevant
12. Updating online catalogues is an example of e-commerce's effect of:
    - elimination of distance and routinization
13. Allowing customers to access a webpage that shows the status of the delivery of their purchase is an example of:
    - managing the customer as a part-time employee
14. The course of action of order, clearance, delivery, and confirming invoice is best described as:
    - payment per debit note
15. A website, hosted by the visitors bureau of city that is a tourist attraction, has a page where recent visitor to the city can post their thoughts and experiences is an example of:
    - using the web to multiply memories
