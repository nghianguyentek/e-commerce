# Assignment Activity Unit 3

This assignment will assess your knowledge and skills about:

1. Apply key concepts of digital marketing, SEO, and social media promotion to a real or hypothetical small business to
   improve its online presence and reach.
2. Reflect on strategies to enhance digital visibility and customer engagement, focusing on practical approaches like
   optimized content, targeted campaigns, and interactive platforms.

Choose a small business or startup (real or hypothetical) and write a 500–700-word reflective response addressing the
following:

1. Explain how the business can improve its online visibility through effective use of digital marketing channels (e.g.,
   website, social media, content marketing).
2. Identify five relevant keywords or phrases that would help the business attract more online visitors. Explain why
   these keywords are suitable and how they could be integrated into website content or blog posts.
3. Suggest two ways the business can use social media or content marketing (e.g., blogs, short videos, or influencer
   posts) to connect and engage with customers. Reflect on how this supports long-term brand loyalty.

Include at least one credible source (textbook, academic article, or industry report) to support your ideas and cite it
using APA 7th edition format.

## Submission Instructions

- Read the rubric on how your assignment will be graded.
- Your assignment should be a minimum of 500 words and not more than 700 words (not including the reference list or the
  title); double-spaced in Times New Roman font, which is no greater than 12 points in size.
- You should use the course readings when creating your assignment. You may also locate and use additional sources
  provided you cite them according to APA with a correct and clickable link.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Digital Presence and Online Visibility: Addresses all aspects of the question with clear understanding and relevant examples demonstrating how a business can enhance online visibility through digital marketing channels.
- SEO and Content Application: Identifies five relevant keywords or phrases and clearly explains their suitability and integration into content, reflecting good understanding of SEO and engagement.
- Use Information effectively: Chooses from a variety of information sources appropriate to the scope of the question. Selects sources after considering the importance to the question/topic of the multiple criteria used (such as relevance to the question, currency, authority, audience, and bias or point of view).
- Sources and Evidence: Demonstrates skillful use of high-quality, credible, relevant sources to develop ideas appropriate for the discipline and genre of the writing. Proper citations and references are consistently included.
- Effective Communication: Uses graceful language that skillfully communicates meaning to readers with clarity and fluency and is error-free.
- Meets the word count range, is double-spaced in Times New Roman font, and font is no greater than 12 points in size.
