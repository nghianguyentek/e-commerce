# Unit 3: Digital Design

In this unit, you will explore the core principles of digital marketing and how businesses build and maintain a strong
online presence in today’s competitive marketplace. You will learn how search engines, social media, and content
creation work together to attract and engage customers effectively. The unit will also introduce you to simple ways of
measuring marketing success using digital analytics tools.

As you progress through this unit, consider the following questions:

1. How do keywords and search engine optimization (SEO) help businesses improve online visibility?
2. What role does social media play in promoting a brand or product?
3. How can basic analytics be used to understand customer engagement and marketing performance?

These competencies will help you develop a clear understanding of how digital tools and strategies enhance
communication, attract customers, and strengthen an organization’s web presence in the e-commerce environment.

## Learning Outcomes

1. Recognize and explain basic concepts of digital marketing and online visibility.
2. Identify and apply effective keywords and phrases for search engine optimization (SEO).
3. Describe simple methods to build and promote a web presence using social media and content marketing.
4. Evaluate the importance of digital channels in customer communication and engagement.

## Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:

1. How do search engines, social media, and online content help businesses increased visibility and reach customers more
   effectively?
2. What role do keywords, blogs, and influencer marketing play in creating a strong digital presence?
3. How can basic web analytics help businesses understand customer behavior and improve marketing performance?
