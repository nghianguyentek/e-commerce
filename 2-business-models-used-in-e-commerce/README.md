# Unit 1: Businesses Models Used in E-Commerce

In this unit, you will explore the different types of business models that drive today’s digital economy. You will learn how online businesses create, deliver, and capture value through traditional and emerging models such as platform-based, ecosystem-driven, and data-powered frameworks. You will also analyze the role of innovation, technology, and artificial intelligence in shaping these business strategies.

As you study this unit, consider the following questions:

1. How do e-commerce business models create value for both businesses and customers?
2. What distinguishes platform and ecosystem models from traditional ones?
3. Why do some digital business models succeed while others fail in the competitive online environment?

These competencies will help you understand how strategic choices and technology integration define success in the e-commerce marketplace.

## Learning Objectives

1. Examine different digital business models (B2B, B2C, C2C, C2B, and D2C) and assess how they generate, share, and retain value online.
2. Compare and evaluate how traditional, platform-based, and ecosystem-driven e-commerce models differ in structure and value creation.
3. Evaluate the role of technology, data analytics, and AI in shaping innovative e-commerce business models.
4. Compare and contrast successful and failed e-commerce models.
