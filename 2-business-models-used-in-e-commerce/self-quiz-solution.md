# Self-Quiz Unit 2 Solution

1. (Multi-choice) Which of the following are included in a definition of a business model:
   - Articulation of a value proposition
   - Identification of a market segment
   - Defined structure to the value chain
   - Estimate cost structure and profit potential
2. A methodology similar to a balance scorecard, but using narratives rather than numeric values is part of which of the following business model frameworks?
   - Intellectual capital statements
3. (Multi-choice) The functionality of a customer relationship management (CRM) include:
   - External interfaces
   - Marketing
   - Sales
   - Service
4. (Multi-choice) Supply chain management (SCM) systems provide the following functionality:
   - Demand and supply planning 
   - Procurement
   - Warehousing
   - Transportation support
   - Supply network collaboration
5. (Multi-choice) Enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems provide the following functionality:
   - Financial accounting
   - Order processing
   - Supply chain management
   - Management Accounting
6. The balanced scorecard tool was a development from which of the following business model frameworks?
   - Strategy maps
7. (Multi-choice) The following elements are included as support activities of a value chain:
   - Firm infrastructure
   - Human resource management
   - Procurement
8. New economics of service is known as:
   - Service-profit chain
9. (Multi-choice) Which of the following are included as business model archetypes:
   - Buyer-seller
   - Network-based
   - Multisided
   - Social community based
   - Consumer-collaboration
10. Linking the infrastructure of a company with its customers most closely describes the following business model framework:
    - Business model canvas

    - Two companies produce kitchen appliances. One company is more high-end than the other
    - Two companies produce laptops. One company prices their laptops lower due to the range is smaller and the design is kept non-customized
12. (Multi-choice) Supplier relationship management (SRM) systems provide the following functionality:
    - Purchase governance
    - Contract management

13. (Multi-choice) The functions of a procurement platform include:
    - Vendor management
    - Purchase order integration

    - Strategic systems auditing
