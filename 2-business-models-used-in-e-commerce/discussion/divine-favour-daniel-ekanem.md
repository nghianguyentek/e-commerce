## Digital Business Models: Creating and Capturing Value in E-Commerce

### 1. Value Creation and Delivery in Amazon’s E-Commerce Model

Successful e-commerce companies create value by using digital technologies to make purchasing faster, more convenient,
and more personalized than many traditional business models. Amazon, for example, combines an online marketplace,
data-driven recommendations, digital payments, fulfillment networks, and third-party sellers. Its platform gives
customers access to a very large product selection while allowing sellers to reach customers without operating their own
large retail stores. Amazon also focuses on price, convenience, fast delivery, and customer service (Amazon.com, Inc.,
2026). This reflects the digital business model described by OpenStax, in which technology connects customer needs with
value creation, delivery, and revenue generation (OpenStax, 2025). Traditional retailers generally depend more heavily
on physical locations, local inventory, and face-to-face transactions.

### 2. Why E-Commerce Models Fail or Thrive

E-commerce businesses thrive when their value proposition matches customer needs, their technology supports a convenient
user experience, and their revenue model can sustain operations. They also need to adapt to changing customer behavior
and competitive conditions (OpenStax, 2025). Amazon demonstrates these strengths through its broad selection,
marketplace model, delivery infrastructure, and multiple revenue sources. In contrast, Quibi provides an example of
failure. The mobile streaming service invested heavily in premium short-form content but struggled to attract enough
paying subscribers. It shut down less than seven months after launch because its standalone business model failed to
gain sufficient customer traction (Faughnder et al., 2020). The comparison shows that strong investment and technology
cannot compensate for weak product-market fit. E-commerce success depends on creating clear customer value and
developing a sustainable way to capture that value.
