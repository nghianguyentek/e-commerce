# Value Creation, Delivery, and Failure Factors in Digital Business Models

by Ali Qassem at 02:06 September 11, 2026

Digital business models create, deliver, and capture value by leveraging technology, data ecosystems, and network
effects, fundamentally altering commercial dynamics compared to traditional brick-and-mortar operations (Laudon &
Traver, 2021).

## Value Creation and Delivery: Amazon vs. Traditional Retail

Unlike physical retailers constrained by floor space and localized inventory, Amazon creates value through vast
selection, competitive pricing, and personalized algorithmic recommendations.

Value delivery occurs through a digitally optimized supply chain and fulfillment infrastructure (Fulfillment by Amazon),
offering rapid, predictable delivery via Prime services (Chaffey, 2019). Traditional businesses rely on physical
storefronts and intermediate intermediaries, whereas Amazon captures value through direct subscription fees, marketplace
seller commissions, and cloud infrastructure services (AWS).

## Thriving vs. Failing E-Commerce Models

- Thriving Model Example (Amazon): Thriving platforms succeed due to strong network effects, high customer retention,
  scalable logistics, and sustainable unit economics. Amazon continually reinvests customer acquisition gains into
  infrastructure, lowering marginal costs and enhancing value capture (Laudon & Traver, 2021).
- Failing Model Example (Kozmo.com): Conversely, digital business models fail when operational expenditures outpace
  customer lifetime value (LTV) or when logistics lack scalability. During the dot-com era, Kozmo.com offered free,
  one-hour delivery for inexpensive convenience items. The model collapsed because fulfillment and delivery costs
  exceeded the gross margin generated per order, demonstrating that digital reach cannot compensate for fundamentally
  unviable unit economics (Chaffey, 2019).

In conclusion, long-term success in the digital economy requires aligning scalable digital value propositions with
disciplined cost structures and sustainable revenue mechanics.
