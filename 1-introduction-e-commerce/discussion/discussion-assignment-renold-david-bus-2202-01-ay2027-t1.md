(2024).Traditional marketing methods, such as print advertising, television commercials, and direct mail, were once the standard. Ursan, G. (2023). Digital marketing offers a unique opportunity to tailor marketing campaigns to target specific customers. **Limitations of Traditional Marketing:**

- **Expensive**: Producing and distributing print or broadcast ads can be costly.
- **Limited targeting**: Ads reach a broad audience, many of whom may not be relevant.
- **Difficult to measure**: It’s hard to track the ROI of traditional campaigns accurately.

**Source**: https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/evolution-marketing-traditional-vs-digital-vijaykumar-swaminathan-ao6hc/

**Advantages of Digital Marketing**

- **Cost-effective**: Suitable for businesses of all sizes, with flexible budgets.
- **Highly targeted**: Ads can be shown to specific demographics based on location, behavior, and interests.
- **Measurable**: Tools like Google Analytics and Facebook Insights provide detailed campaign performance data.
- **Real-time engagement**: Businesses can interact with customers through comments, likes, and direct messages.

**Source**: https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/evolution-marketing-traditional-vs-digital-vijaykumar-swaminathan-ao6hc/

**Conclusion**

The Internet and emerging digital technologies have changed both how businesses operate and how customers interact with them. Businesses can now reach customers globally, provide faster and more personalized services, and use data to better understand customer needs. Marketing has similarly evolved from traditional, one-way communication toward digital and inbound approaches that engage customers through valuable content, social media, search engines, email, and personalized experiences. As technology continues to develop, businesses that effectively embrace digital tools and maintain a customer-centered approach will be better positioned to attract, satisfy, and retain customers in the modern marketplace.
