## Discussion Task Unit 1

1. How have the Internet and emerging digital technologies such as AI, mobile apps, or social media changed the way businesses interact with and serve their customers?

Digital technologies have transformed businesses, enabling them to interact with customers in a fast, efficient, and round-the-clock manner, anytime and anywhere, (The Evolution of Marketing: From Traditional to Digital - PHENYX, 2024). Sébastien Ronteau et al. state that consumers used to rent DVD movies, which had to be returned by a specific time to avoid late fees. Netflix solved this issue by allowing consumers to keep the DVD as long as they want through a subscription model, then return it when done. The company upgraded to online streaming, which beat movie theaters and video rental companies. Digital tools also enable businesses to meet their consumers on platforms to gain feedback, predict their preferences, and solve problems instantly. Phone apps let users book services, shop, order meals, and even track travel destinations, among others. We live in an age where, as long as one has a digital device, almost anything can be accomplished.

In what ways has marketing evolved from traditional methods to inbound and digital strategies that attract and retain customers online?

Traditional marketing methods reached out to consumers through billboard advertising, radio, and TV commercials without interaction with consumers. The digital approach provides social media platforms and blogs, with two-way communication where consumers can comment, share, and respond to each other,

Services and products are sold instantly as well as upgraded based on collective data of consumers' purchasing preferences. This information is accessed quickly through consumers' internet browsing history. The traditional marketing approach pushed out its advertising, while the digital approach brings in consumers’ purchasing habits for the business to provide them with what they look for.
