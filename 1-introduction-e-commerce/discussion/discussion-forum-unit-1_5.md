Digital transformation has changed the way businesses communicate with customers and provide their services. In the past, communication was mostly one-way. For example, companies used television, radio, newspapers, and billboards to send advertising messages to a large audience. Today, the Internet allows customers and businesses to communicate directly with each other. Customers can ask questions, write reviews, compare products, and share their experiences on social media. Digital marketing also gives businesses the opportunity to understand their customers through data and to create more personalized experiences (Red & Yellow, 2022).

Emerging technologies have also improved customer service. Mobile apps allow customers to shop, make payments, communicate with companies, and receive services from almost anywhere. Artificial intelligence can help businesses analyze customer information, make recommendations, and provide faster responses. Digital technologies also make marketing activities more measurable because companies can analyze customer interactions and use this information to improve their decisions (Red & Yellow, 2022).

Marketing has also changed from mainly pushing advertisements toward customers to attracting them through useful and relevant content. This is an important idea behind inbound marketing. Instead of only telling people to buy something, businesses can provide educational articles, videos, social media posts, and other useful information. Content marketing focuses on creating content that responds to customers' needs at different stages of their buying journey (Red & Yellow, 2022). In my opinion, this approach can help businesses build stronger relationships because customers receive value before and after making a purchase.
