# Impact of Digital Technologies on Business and Marketing

by Francis Shaba (Sep 4, 2026 04:00)

## Discussion

The Internet and emerging digital technologies have changed how businesses interact with and serve their customers. In the past, businesses mainly depended on face-to-face communication, telephone calls, television, radio, and printed advertising. Today, businesses can communicate with customers instantly through websites, mobile apps, social media, and online messaging. Technologies such as artificial intelligence (AI) also help businesses understand customer needs and provide more personalised services. For example, companies can use customer data to recommend products, answer questions through chatbots, and improve the overall customer experience. Digital platforms have also allowed businesses to reach customers globally and operate through new business models such as subscriptions and online marketplaces (Ronteau et al., 2023).

Marketing has also changed from traditional methods to inbound and digital strategies. Traditional marketing often involves sending advertisements to a large audience through newspapers, television, radio, and billboards. Digital marketing is more interactive, allowing businesses to reach specific audiences using search engines, social media, email, websites, and mobile platforms. Inbound marketing focuses on attracting customers by providing useful and relevant content rather than simply pushing advertisements. Content such as blogs, videos, social media posts, and helpful website information can build trust and encourage customers to engage with a business. Businesses can also use analytics to understand customer behaviour and improve their marketing strategies. According to Solomons, Kliphuis, and Wadley (2022), effective digital marketing depends on understanding the audience, providing useful content, and creating a good user experience. Overall, digital technologies have made business communication more direct, personalised, interactive, and customer-focused.
