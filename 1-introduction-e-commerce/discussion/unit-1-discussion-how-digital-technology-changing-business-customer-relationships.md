# Unit 1 Discussion : How Digital Technology Is Changing Business and Customer Relationships

by Pascale Liautaud (Sep 7, 2026)

Digital technologies have transformed how businesses operate and interact with customers worldwide. Ronteau et al. (2023) explain that digital businesses can “create, propose, deliver, and capture value” (p. 1). These changes have reshaped business models and customer experiences globally.

The Internet has allowed businesses to move beyond the traditional business model, where the company was the main creator of value, to one in which customers, providers, and the company are involved in creating value. Ronteau et al. (2023) explain that digital technology creates opportunities to engage participants as “value creators and/or recipients” (p. 8). Digital platforms such as Spotify demonstrate this change by connecting listeners, artists, and advertisers, allowing each to contribute to the business ecosystem (Ronteau et al., 2023). Digital platforms also allow businesses to interact directly with customers and offer more personalized experiences using customer data. Furthermore, businesses and customers can connect regardless of geographic location or time zone. For example, a small business in Haiti can use a digital platform to reach a customer in Paris without being limited by physical location or traditional business hours.

The Internet has shifted marketing from traditional approaches that push messages toward customers to digital strategies that allow consumers and brands to connect directly through online platforms (Solomons et al., 2022). “Content marketing is an umbrella term that focuses on matching content (information, inspiration, or entertainment) to your customer needs” (Solomons et al., 2022, p. 247). By providing relevant content, businesses can attract, engage, and retain audiences. Digital technologies also allow businesses to create more accurate customer personas, develop relevant content, analyze results, and adjust their strategies.

Overall, digital technologies have transformed both how businesses create value and how they build lasting relationships with customers.
