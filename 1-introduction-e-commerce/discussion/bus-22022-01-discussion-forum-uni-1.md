## Digital Transformation and the Evolution of Marketing

### 1. Impact of the Internet and Emerging Digital Technologies

The Internet and digital technologies have significantly changed how businesses interact with customers. Websites, mobile applications, social media, and artificial intelligence (AI) allow businesses to communicate with customers quickly and across multiple channels. Mobile applications, for example, allow customers to purchase products, make payments, track orders, and receive support without visiting a physical location. Social media also allows businesses to communicate directly with customers, respond to questions, receive feedback, and build relationships. AI further improves customer experiences by analyzing customer data and supporting personalized recommendations, automated services, and faster decision-making (IBM, 2024). These technologies have therefore made customer interactions more convenient, personalized, and continuous.

### 2. Evolution From Traditional to Inbound and Digital Marketing

Marketing has also shifted from traditional approaches, such as television, radio, newspapers, and direct mail, toward digital and inbound strategies. Traditional marketing generally pushes promotional messages toward a broad audience, while inbound marketing focuses on attracting customers through useful and relevant content (HubSpot, 2026). Businesses now use search engine optimization, social media, email marketing, blogs, videos, and personalized content to reach customers who are actively searching for information or solutions. HubSpot (2026) describes inbound marketing through the stages of attracting, engaging, and delighting customers. Digital analytics also allow businesses to measure customer behavior and improve marketing campaigns based on actual data. This shift has made marketing more targeted, interactive, measurable, and customer-centered.
