The Internet and developing digital technologies have transformed the way businesses engage with and service their customers. Mobile apps, social media, artificial intelligence (AI), and digital platforms enable businesses to engage with customers in real time, personalise experiences, and provide services beyond regional borders. According to Ronteau et al. (2023), digital business models generate value through connectivity, data, personalisation, and platform-based interactions. Businesses, for example, can leverage customer data and AI to offer items, respond to questions via chatbots, and provide faster customer service. Social media also enables businesses to communicate with customers, solicit feedback, and create online communities.

Marketing has similarly evolved from traditional methods such as television, radio, newspapers, and billboards toward inbound and digital strategies. Traditional marketing generally communicates messages to broad audiences, whereas inbound marketing focuses on attracting customers by providing useful and relevant content. Solomons et al. (2022) emphasize the importance of digital marketing, user experience, content marketing, and multiple digital channels in reaching today's consumers. Businesses can use websites, blogs, social media, email, videos, and search engines to attract potential customers and maintain relationships with existing ones.

As a result of the digital shift, marketing is now more interactive, personalised, and measurable. Businesses can analyse customer behaviour and use the results to improve products, content, and services. Overall, good digital marketing is more than just promoting a product; it is also about creating value, providing positive customer experiences, and developing long-term relationships with customers online.
