**Digital Transformation and the Evolution of Marketing**

The Internet and emerging digital technologies have fundamentally changed how businesses create value and interact with customers. Smartphones, mobile applications, artificial intelligence (AI), social media, and digital platforms allow businesses to provide faster, more personalized, and continuous customer experiences. For example, companies can use customer data and AI to recommend products, automate support through chatbots, and personalize content based on individual preferences. Ronteau et al. (2023) explain that digital business models create new mechanisms for value creation and capture, while platforms and connectivity enable businesses to reach customers across geographical boundaries. Similarly, digital technologies have made customer experience more interactive because customers can communicate with businesses, compare alternatives, provide reviews, and complete transactions through multiple digital channels (Lemon & Verhoef, 2016).

Marketing has also shifted significantly from traditional methods such as television, radio, newspapers, and direct mail toward inbound and digital strategies. Traditional marketing generally pushed promotional messages toward broad audiences, whereas inbound marketing focuses on attracting customers by providing useful and relevant content. Search engine optimization, social media marketing, email marketing, content marketing, and personalized online advertising allow businesses to reach specific customer segments and measure engagement more effectively. Solomons et al. (2022) emphasize the importance of digital marketing, user experience, content strategy, and multi-channel commerce in creating effective digital customer journeys. Kannan and Li (2017) further explain that digital marketing enables firms to use data and multiple digital touchpoints throughout the customer journey. Consequently, successful businesses increasingly focus not only on attracting customers but also on building long-term relationships through personalized experiences, valuable content, and continuous engagement.
