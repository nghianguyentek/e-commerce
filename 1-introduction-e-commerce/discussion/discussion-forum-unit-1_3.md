With social media, businesses are able to read comments or hear the opinions of consumers through videos that are made on platforms such as TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter (Solomons et al., 2022). This information could help businesses understand what consumers think about their products and their company as a whole. Companies can respond directly to comments and direct messages, creating a more personal relationship with people. I believe this direct contact could create trust with consumers because they may feel like the company is really engaging. Social media also allows businesses to digitally market their company and products on various platforms to create a consistent connection with customers and expand their core audience (Solomons et al., 2022).

2. The need for companies to market in the traditional way through television, magazines, radio, and newspapers has decreased since the surge of the internet (Solomons et al., 2022). Most people use streaming platforms to watch shows, so businesses have to use digital marketing to connect with a wider audience. Digital marketing does this because companies can pinpoint their target audience and market more to them on different platforms. They can attract customers by having a social media presence on many different platforms, and they can retain their audience by meeting supply and demand.
