Throughout the world and across industies, digital technologies have come to fundamentally reshape consumer experiences and the approaches that businesses make to reach new customers. The shift from traditional approaches to inbound and digital marketing has redefined the business-consumer relationship, improving access to communication, shopping, and customer support. As a result of these changes, organizations are depending more on online content and digital channels to connect with their target audiences,and are leveraging technology as the cornerstone for establishing and sustaining customer relationships.

The Internet and emerging digital technologies such as AI, mobile apps, and social media have changed the way businesses interact with and serve their customers by personalizing customer experiences, allowing for greater convenience and accessibility, and allowing for direct customer interaction. With AI, businesses can analyze individual purchase history, browsing behavior, and customer reviews to personalize recommendations and improve customer satisfaction (Stryker & Kavlakoglu, 2024). With mobile apps and social media, businesses allow customers to purchase products directly from their phone's home screen and interact with the brand on social media, while building communities that seek out a strong connection to the brands they know and love (Baker, 2019).  Marketing has evolved from traditional methods such as TV and radio advertising, cold calling, direct mail, and mailed loyalty program offers to targeted marketing via social media and email lists, inbound marketing driven by individual content creators, and building ongoing digital relationships through rewards programs built into applications.
