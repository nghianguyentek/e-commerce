The Internet and emerging digital technologies have changed business–customer relationships from slow, mostly one-way communication into faster and more interactive experiences. In my opinion, one of the most important benefits is the improvement of customer service. Customers can contact companies through mobile applications, social media, online chat, or AI-powered chatbots instead of waiting a long time for a telephone response. Artificial intelligence can also provide product recommendations based on customer preferences, answer routine questions, and offer automated support at any time, saving time for both customers and businesses.
Mobile applications have had the greatest effect on my daily life because they allow me to buy groceries, clothes, and other products, communicate with doctors, obtain medication, and complete banking or government services. Online shopping has also removed geographical barriers: customers can purchase products from another country, pay electronically, and arrange delivery or returns without visiting a physical store. These services create value by making products and services more accessible and convenient (Ronteau et al., 2023).
Marketing has also changed significantly. Traditional marketing primarily pushes promotional messages to broad audiences through channels such as television, newspapers, or billboards. In contrast, inbound marketing attracts customers by providing relevant and useful content that encourages them to engage with the business. Digital marketing supports this approach by allowing companies to target specific audiences, personalise messages, and measure campaign performance more accurately (Solomons et al., 2022). From my perspective, social media is highly effective because it presents product information, prices, discounts, and services where customers already spend their time. Personally, free delivery, discount vouchers, fast customer service, and a strong reputation encourage me to purchase and remain loyal to a brand.
