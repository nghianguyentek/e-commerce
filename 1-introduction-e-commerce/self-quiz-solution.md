# Self-Quiz Unit 1 Solution

1. Innovation risk is most closely described as:
   - Failure to adapt
2. Business Web Alliance is most closely described as:
   - Innovation in products and processes, trust building between different actors, abstinence of hierarchical supervision
3. Actions that lead to an intent reaction on the part of a visitor is known as:
   - Call to action
4. Business Web Integrator is most closely described as:
   - Systematic supplier selection, process optimization for total value chain
5. (Multi-choice) Which of the following are organizational gains made possible by e-commerce?
   - Profitability
   - Increased market share
   - Improving service
   - Faster delivery of product
6. Business services infrastructure is most closely described as:
   - Supports the secure transmission of credit card numbers and electronic fund transfer (EFT)
7. Business Web Aggregator is most closely described as:
   - Presentation of a large variety of products, fixed prices and not negotiation between supplier and customer - simple fulfillment from customer's point of view
8. (Multi-choice) E-commerce might employ the following:
   - Online shopping websites for retail sales direct to customers
   - Business-to-business buying and selling
   - Gathering and using demographic data through web contacts and social media
   - Engaging in "pretail" for launching new products and services
   - Business-to-business electronic data interchange
9. Which of the following products would benefit THE MOST by e-commerce?
   - Consumer electronics
10. (Multi-choice) The main features of M-commerce include:
    - Location independence of (mobile) customers
    - Increased computing power of mobile devices
    - Security
    - Accessibility of customers
11. Text within the link is known as:
    - Anchor text
12. (Multi-choice) E-commerce is built on which of the following technology layers?
    - Electronic commerce applications
    - Business service infrastructure
    - Electronic publishing infrastructure
    - Message distribution infrastructure
    - National information structure`
13. (Multi-choice) The following are main advantages to the electronic exchange of standard business documents between firms are:
    - Paper handling is reduced
    - Data are exchanged in real time
    - Fewer errors since data are entered once
    - Enhanced data sharing enabling greater coordination among partners
    - Accelerated payments and money flows
14. (Multi-choice) The following are characteristics of e-cash:
    - Required a digital bank account
    - Requires a password for use
15. (Multi-choice) The following are methods of an electronic payment:
    - Electronic fund transfer
    - Digital cash
    - E-cash
    - Credit card
