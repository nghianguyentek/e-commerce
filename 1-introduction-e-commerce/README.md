# Introduction to E-Commerce

In this first unit, you will explore how the Internet and emerging digital technologies—such as mobile commerce, social
media, and artificial intelligence—have transformed the way businesses operate and engage with customers. You will also
learn about the shift from traditional to inbound and digital marketing approaches and what makes an effective online
presence.

As you go through this unit, consider the following questions:

1. How has digital transformation changed the way businesses reach and serve their customers?
2. What elements make an e-commerce website effective and user-friendly?
3. What basic infrastructure — such as websites, analytics tools, and digital platforms — supports the operations of an
   online business?

These competencies will help you build a strong foundation for understanding how technology, marketing, and user
experience work together in the digital economy.

## Learning Outcomes

1. Discuss how the Internet and digital technologies have transformed business models and customer experiences globally.
2. Explain the shift from traditional to inbound and digital marketing approaches.
3. Identify the core components that make an effective and user-friendly online presence in e-commerce.
4. Describe basic e-commerce infrastructure such as websites, databases, and analytics tools supporting online
   operations.
