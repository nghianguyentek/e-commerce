# Self-Quiz Unit 6 Solution

1. Yelp is most closely described as:
   - social networking site that provides reviews and discussions related to services in the food, restaurant, and entertainment venues.
2. LinkedIn is most closely described as:
   - a social media venue for individual seeking to network with a career focus.
3. (Multiple choice) Primary advantages to Facebook Business Manager include:
   - eliminates mix-ups between personal accounts and page administrations
   - allows businesses to manage page roles and ad accounts access from one convenient place
   - allows a business to see which pages and ad accounts have been assigned to a particular individual
   - allows a business to see a list of people working on a particular page or ad account
   - allows partners and affiliated ad agencies to request access to pages and ad accounts
4. Georgetown University sponsors an international trip that is available only to Georgetown alumni. This would be an example of the following type of web attractor:
   - The club
5. It ___ to imitate a search engine.
   - takes some effort
6. Foursquare/Swarm is most closely described as:
   - a social media application specifically designed for business where data analytics involving customer check-in and loyalty program memberships.
7. Free recipes from Ragu would be an example of the following type of web attractor:
   - The gift shop
8. The letters, "BB&T" on a prominent building in a city where this bank has a business presence in an example of the following way of developing customer loyalty:
   - Signs, logos, or brands
9. Waze is most closely described as:
   - a community-driven social media application that gathers map data and traffic information.
10. A web site that engages visitors in game activities that demand a high degree of participation is an example of the following type of web attractor:
    - The entertainment park
11. It ___ to imitate a corporate brochure.
    - is easy
12. It ___ to imitate an advanced customer service application.
    - is costly
13. The website of Greenfield Village (a museum built by the Ford Motor Company) is an example of the following type of web attractor:
    - The archive
14. If the Genworth Company was the sole promoter the Richmond Virginia Folk Festival is an example of the following type of web attractor:
    - The exclusive sponsorship
15. A web site that specializes in determining (primarily for Americans) the ancestry of its visitors would be an example of the following type of web attractor:
    - The freeway intersection or portal
