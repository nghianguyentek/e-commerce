# Unit 6: Social Media and Business

In this unit, you will explore how social media shapes modern e-commerce by influencing customer engagement, brand visibility, and online communication. You will learn how different platforms support marketing activities, how businesses attract and retain customers through digital interaction, and how basic customer relationship tools help manage feedback and build loyalty. The unit also highlights the importance of responsible and ethical communication in maintaining trust online.
