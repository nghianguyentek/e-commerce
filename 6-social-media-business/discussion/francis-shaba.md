# Nike, Inc.: Social Media, CRM, and Ethical Practice

posted Oct 10, 2026 21:15

## 1. Visibility and engagement through social media

Social media helps businesses reach large audiences, build relationships, and encourage interaction (Solomons et al., 2022). Nike's "Dream Crazy" campaign (2018) is a strong example. It used Instagram and X (Twitter), platforms suited to short, visual, emotional content shared quickly (McHaney & Sachs, 2016). Nike used a bold hashtag (#JustDoIt), inspiring storytelling with athlete Colin Kaepernick, and video content that invited sharing. Research on the campaign shows that purpose-driven messaging shaped how stakeholders perceived the brand and strengthened its emotional connection with audiences (Aniskova, 2020). The campaign generated massive earned media, growing Nike's online community and brand visibility (Solomons et al., 2022). Nike also uses community management to respond to followers, turning audiences into active brand advocates (Ronteau et al., 2023).

## 2. Low-cost strategies for small e-commerce businesses

First, user-generated content (UGC): encourage customers to post photos with a branded hashtag. This is cheap, builds trust, and creates authentic social proof (McHaney & Sachs, 2016).

Second, email plus social integration: grow a mailing list via a free sign-up incentive and send consistent, personalised messages (Solomons et al., 2022).

Both work because they cost little but build loyal, repeat customers. A basic CRM or analytics tool, such as Google Analytics or Mailchimp, tracks customer behaviour, clicks, and feedback, helping the business respond quickly and personalise offers (Solomons et al., 2022).

## 3. Ethical communication practice

A key ethical practice is transparency and honest use of customer data. Businesses must clearly explain how they collect and use personal information and avoid manipulative attention-driven tactics (Ronteau et al., 2023). This matters because trust is the foundation of a long-term loyalty; customers who feel respected are more likely to stay and recommend the brand (Solomons et al., 2022).
