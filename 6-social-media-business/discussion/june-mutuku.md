## Social Media and Business

Social media helps businesses increase visibility by quickly reaching large audiences and allowing customers to like, comment, share, and discuss content. It also supports direct interaction and feedback, helping businesses build customer relationships (Solomons et al., 2022). A clear example is Nike’s “The Chosen” campaign, which used Facebook, a social networking platform. Facebook suited Nike because it encouraged brand promotion, customer participation, and community interaction. Nike used video content, a video contest, customer submissions, sharing, and grassroots events to create conversations and increase visibility (Ramaswamy, 2008). These tactics turned customers into active participants, increased engagement, and strengthened Nike's relationships with its audience.

Small e-commerce businesses can use low-cost social media strategies such as user-generated content and regular promotional posts to attract and retain customers. User-generated content, such as customer reviews, photos, and testimonials, builds trust because potential buyers can see real experiences. Promotional posts featuring discounts, new products, or limited-time offers can attract attention and encourage purchases. A basic CRM tool, such as HubSpot CRM, can help businesses record customer interactions, track feedback, and respond to questions or complaints promptly. Social media analytics can also show which posts receive the most engagement, helping businesses understand customer preferences and improve future content and marketing decisions.

An important ethical practice is to communicate honestly and use customer data responsibly. Businesses should avoid misleading claims and be transparent about how customer information is collected and used. This is important because data-driven digital business models create ethical concerns around attention and personal data (Ronteau et al., 2023). Responsible communication builds confidence and helps customers trust the business over the long term.
