## Social Media Marketing and Customer Relationships in E-Commerce

### 1.Social Media Visibility and Customer Engagement

Social media helps businesses increase brand awareness, communicate with customers, and encourage purchases. Nike, for example, uses Instagram, a visual social media platform, to showcase products, share athlete stories, and connect its brand with fitness and sports communities. Visual content, hashtags, customer interactions, and promotional campaigns can help businesses reach audiences and encourage engagement. These activities support brand recognition and direct potential customers toward products.

### 2. Low-Cost Strategies and Customer Relationship Management

Small e-commerce businesses can use two affordable strategies. First, they can publish short product demonstration videos and customer testimonials. This content helps customers understand products and evaluate their usefulness before purchasing. Second, businesses can encourage repeat purchases through interactive posts, personalized responses, and customer loyalty offers. These activities strengthen relationships and encourage customer retention.

A tool such as HubSpot can help businesses organize customer interactions, monitor social media engagement, and identify feedback that requires a response. Social analytics can also reveal which posts generate more engagement, helping businesses improve their content strategy (HubSpot, n.d.).

### 3. Ethical and Responsible Communication

Businesses should disclose paid partnerships and influencer sponsorships clearly. The Federal Trade Commission (FTC, 2019) explains that influencers should make their relationships with brands obvious to audiences. Honest disclosures help customers distinguish advertising from independent recommendations. This transparency supports informed purchasing decisions and reduces the risk of misleading customers. By communicating honestly and responding respectfully to complaints, businesses can strengthen customer trust and support long-term relationships.
