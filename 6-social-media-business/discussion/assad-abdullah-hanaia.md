# Social Media Marketing and Customer Relationships

posted Oct 11, 2026 03:45

I believe social media helps businesses increase visibility by encouraging customers to share experiences and interact directly with brands. A good example is Coca-Cola’s Share a Coke campaign, which personalized bottles with names and encouraged customers to share photos and stories using the hashtag #ShareACoke. The company used social platforms, including Instagram, to help people share personalized bottles with friends (The Coca-Cola Company, 2014). I think this strategy works because customers become participants in the campaign rather than passive viewers of advertisements.

For small e-commerce businesses, I would use two low-cost strategies. First, I would post short product demonstration videos and encourage buyers to share honest reviews. This helps potential customers understand the product before purchasing. Second, I would respond quickly to comments and messages and offer returning customers relevant discounts. To manage feedback, I would use a spreadsheet to record complaints, questions, and follow-up actions. For example, if several customers report unclear sizing information, I would improve product descriptions. This connects social media engagement with customer relationship management and supports stronger customer loyalty (Solomons et al., 2022).

I also believe honest communication is essential for maintaining customer trust. Businesses should clearly disclose paid influencer partnerships and avoid fake reviews or misleading claims. The Federal Trade Commission (2019) explains that influencers should make their relationships with brands clear to their audiences. If customers discover that a recommendation was secretly paid for, they may question the company’s honesty. Therefore, I would prioritize transparency and respond respectfully to criticism because long-term customer loyalty depends on trust, not simply attracting clicks or making immediate sales.
