## Discussion Forum Unit 6: Social Media and Business

posted 22:27 Oct 10, 2026

Social media has become one of the most effective tools for increasing business visibility because it allows companies to communicate directly with customers while promoting products in real time. A good example is SafeBoda Uganda, which primarily uses Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp to market its transport, food delivery, and parcel services. These platforms suit SafeBoda because they reach young, mobile-first users who spend significant time on social media. The company uses short videos, customer testimonials, promotional hashtags such as campaign-specific offers, paid advertisements, and active community management by responding to customer questions and complaints, which strengthens customer engagement and brand awareness (Solomons et al., 2022).

For small e-commerce businesses in Uganda, two affordable strategies can produce meaningful results. First, posting consistent short-form videos and product demonstrations on TikTok and Instagram Reels helps businesses reach new audiences organically. Second, using WhatsApp Business to send product updates, answer customer inquiries, and provide after-sales support builds stronger customer relationships at little cost. These approaches encourage interaction and repeat purchases because customers receive timely and personalized communication.

A basic CRM and analytics tool such as Meta Business Suite can help businesses monitor comments, messages, reach, engagement, and customer feedback from Facebook and Instagram in one place. By reviewing these insights, businesses can identify popular content, respond quickly to customer concerns, and improve future marketing campaigns (Solomons et al., 2022).

An important ethical communication practice is being transparent and protecting customer privacy. Businesses should avoid misleading advertisements, clearly disclose sponsored content, and handle customers' personal information responsibly. Ethical communication builds credibility and trust, encouraging customers to remain loyal and confidently engage with the brand over time (Ronteau et al., 2023).
