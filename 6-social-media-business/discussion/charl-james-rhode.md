# Discussion Unit 6 - Social Media Marketing

posted Oct 11, 2026 21:09

Social media helps businesses increase brand visibility, communicate directly with customers, and encourage engagement through interactive content. Solomons et al. (2022) explain that effective social media marketing requires selecting appropriate platforms and planning content around business objectives.

A good example is Coca-Cola’s Share a Coke campaign. The company encouraged customers to share personalized bottles and photographs online using the hashtag #ShareACoke. Platforms such as Instagram and Facebook supported visual storytelling and sharing among friends, helping customers participate in promoting the brand (The Coca-Cola Company, 2014). This approach demonstrates how user-generated content can increase brand awareness and create personal connections with customers.

Small e-commerce businesses can use two low-cost strategies to attract and retain customers. First, they can publish useful, engaging content, including product demonstrations, short videos, and customer testimonials. Second, they can encourage repeat purchases through personalized offers and responsive customer service. These strategies support customer loyalty by providing value and making customers feel appreciated (Solomons et al., 2022).

A basic customer relationship management (CRM) tool, such as HubSpot CRM, can help businesses record customer enquiries, organize feedback, and track interactions. Businesses can also use platform analytics to monitor engagement, reach, and clicks, helping them identify which content performs best. These insights support better marketing decisions and stronger customer relationships.

Finally, businesses should communicate ethically by protecting customer privacy and obtaining appropriate consent before collecting or using personal information. Ronteau et al. (2023) discuss ethical concerns associated with data-driven business models. Respecting privacy and communicating transparently helps businesses establish credibility, strengthen customer trust, and encourage long-term loyalty.
