# Discussion Unit 6

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:

- How do businesses use social media to promote products and engage with customers?
- What strategies help attract and retain customers in digital environments?
- Why are ethical communication and responsible online behavior essential for building trust?

## Task

Using this unit’s readings and examples, pick at least one real company or campaign and respond to the following:

- How does social media help businesses increase visibility and engage customers?  Provide one clear example (company or campaign), identify the type of social media platform used and explain why it suits the business's goals, and describe the specific social tactics used (e.g., content, hashtags, paid ads, community management).
- What practical, low-cost strategies can small e-commerce businesses use on social media to attract and retain customers? Suggest two tactics, explain why they work, and explain how at least one basic CRM or analytics tool can help the business track and respond to customer feedback.
- Based on the readings, describe one ethical or responsible communication practice that online businesses should follow when using social media. Why is this important for building long-term customer trust?
