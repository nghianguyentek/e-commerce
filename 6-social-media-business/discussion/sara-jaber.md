Social media helps businesses reach wider audiences and communicate with customers through likes, comments, shares, and customer-created content. Audience data can also help platforms show relevant content to users, increasing engagement (Ronteau et al., 2023).

A suitable example from the reading is Coca-Cola. Its brand essence is connected with happiness, so the company could use Instagram to publish visual content about enjoyment, fun, and sharing. Instagram suits this purpose because images and short videos can communicate these themes effectively. Coca-Cola could also use a branded hashtag, encourage customers to share experiences, and respond to comments. These tactics could increase visibility and connect customer participation with the brand’s identity (Solomons et al., 2022).

For a small e-commerce business, one low-cost tactic is encouraging customers to share product reviews, photographs, or videos. This creates authentic content and may increase trust. Another tactic is publishing useful posts, such as product explanations, answers to common questions, and advice. Responding quickly to comments and messages can improve the customer experience. Loyalty depends not only on rewards but also on emotional connection, personal attention, and consistent service (Shep Hyken, 2016). A tool such as Meta Business Suite can track reach, comments, shares, clicks, and messages, helping the business understand customer feedback.

In my opinion, product transparency is an important ethical practice. Companies should clearly explain product features, size, materials, use, full price, extra fees, and return, exchange, and delivery policies. This helps customers make suitable decisions and feel respected. This is especially important on social media, where attractive content can influence customers. Honest details reduce misunderstandings and complaints, while showing that the business values long-term relationships more than sales. Transparency can build trust, encourage repeat purchases, and motivate positive recommendations (Ronteau et al., 2023).
