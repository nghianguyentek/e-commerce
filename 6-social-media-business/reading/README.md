# Reading Assignment Unit 6

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:

- How do businesses use social media to promote products and engage with customers?
- What strategies help attract and retain customers in digital environments?
- Why are ethical communication and responsible online behavior essential for building trust?
