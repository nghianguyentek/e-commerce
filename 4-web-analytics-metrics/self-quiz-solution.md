# Self-Quiz Unit 4 Solution

1. (Multichoice) An effective website presence can contribute to marketing communications by:
   - providing product information
   - gaining access to heretofore unknown buying influences
   - projecting a favorable corporate image
2. The specific steps (stages) of the control cycle are in the following order:
   - Target agreement, planning, realization and monitoring, reporting, deviation analysis and forecast, communications, and actions and monitoring
3. The capacity to turn purchasers into returning purchasers is most closely described as:
   - retention efficiency
4. (Multichoice) After a website has been well established, the following items should have been accomplished:
   - The brand is visually reflective of its values
   - The website is constructed to allow ease of editing
   - The commercial enterprise has a plan for the next set of content
   - A plan has been established regarding who will manage the site's SEO
5. 'Hits' on site divided by people aware of the site is most closely described as:
   - attractability efficiency
6. (Multichoice) Common characteristics between shopping on the web and shopping in an open, physical community market include:
   - openness
   - informality
   - interactivity
7. ___ serves the analysis of visitor properties and visitor behavior to consecutively optimize visitor and customer focus, customer exploitation, development and loyalty.
   - Web user performance management
8. Easy links and clicks to other site pages facilitate effective:
   - Navigation
9. Effective calls to actions are most closely described as:
   - Powerful CTAs
10. Maximizing the locatability of a site is most closely described as:
    - Attractability efficiency
