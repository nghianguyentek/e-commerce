# Assignment Activity Unit 7

This assignment will assess your knowledge and skills about:

- Identify common security threats and privacy risks in online businesses.
- Explain simple ways to protect digital data and build customer trust.
- Recognize key privacy rules and compliance standards.
- Apply basic safety measures for secure online transactions.

## Task

Choose one online business or platform (examples: Amazon, Flipkart, Shopify stores, Paytm, Uber, Bank apps, or any local business that sells online). Write a 500–700-word reflective response addressing the following:

### 1. Analysis of Security Threats and Privacy Risks

- Identify at least two security threats or privacy risks commonly faced by the chosen company (e.g., phishing, weak passwords, data breaches, unsafe networks, unencrypted transactions).
- Explain why these threats are dangerous and how they can damage customer trust or disrupt business operations.
- Discuss one real incident (from your chosen company or a similar company) that illustrates how such threats affect online businesses.

### 2. Practical Data Protection and Trust-Building Measures 

- Describe the key protective measures the company uses to safeguard customer information (e.g., SSL certificates, encryption, 2FA/OTP, secure checkout pages, privacy notices).
- Reflect on how these measures contribute to building customer trust and reducing risk.

### 3. Privacy Compliance

- Name one privacy law or compliance standard relevant to your chosen company and briefly explain why it matters for protecting customer data online.

## Submission Instructions

- Read the rubric on how your assignment will be graded.
- Your assignment should be a minimum of 500 words and not more than 700 words (not including the reference list or the title); double-spaced in Times New Roman font, which is no greater than 12 points in size.
- You should use the course readings when creating your assignment. You may also locate and use additional sources provided you cite them according to APA with a correct and clickable link.
- Remember to cite and list references to validate statements of fact or information that is not considered common knowledge and to corroborate personal opinions/points of view

## Evaluation Criteria

- Analysis of Security Threats and Privacy Risks: Clearly identifies two or more specific security threats or privacy risks, with detailed explanation of why they are dangerous to customer trust and business operations, supported by a highly relevant real incident applied to the chosen company's context.
- Protective Measures and Trust-Building Strategies: Provides a thorough explanation of specific protective measures (e.g., SSL, encryption, 2FA) applied to the chosen company's context, with clear and well-reasoned reflection on how these measures reduce risk and build customer trust in secure online transactions.
- Privacy Compliance: Correctly names a specific and relevant privacy law or compliance standard with a clear and well-reasoned explanation of why it matters for protecting customer data online, applied to the chosen company's context.
