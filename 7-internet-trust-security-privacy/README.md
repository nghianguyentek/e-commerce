# Unit 7: Internet Trust, Security, and Privacy

In this unit, you will explore the security and privacy foundations that protect customers, businesses, and digital assets in online environments. You will learn about the most common threats faced by online businesses, including data breaches, phishing attacks, and other vulnerabilities that can compromise trust. The unit also explains practical ways to safeguard digital information, from encryption and secure passwords to safe browsing and data protection practices. 

As you move through this unit, consider the following questions:

- What are the most common security and privacy risks that online businesses must watch for?
- How do simple protection strategies, such as strong authentication and secure data handling, build trust with customers?
- Why are privacy regulations and compliance requirements essential to safe and ethical online transactions?

These competencies will help you understand how security awareness, privacy protection, and compliance practices create trustworthy digital experiences and support the long-term success of e-commerce businesses.
